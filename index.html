Why Burka requests Google access

The personal installation connects accounts explicitly authorized by the owner through Google's REST APIs. Reading and optional writing capabilities have separate OAuth grants.

  • Calendar supports reading primary-calendar events. A separate optional grant permits updating the description of a configured existing reminder instance after a revision check.
  • Gmail supports message searches and reading selected messages. Separately authorized sending requires the owner's confirmation of the exact account, recipients, subject and text for each email.
  • Drive supports searching and reading selected files, and create Google Docs in owner-selected folders when that capability is authorized.
  • Sheets supports reading and editing bounded spreadsheet ranges through a separately authorized capability.

Email previews, permission records and send receipts are stored privately on the owner's application host. Selected Google results may be included in an AI-assisted task, as explained in the AI services section of the privacy notice.

Personal use, with explicit connections

Burka is maintained for one person's own use. There is no public registration, hosted assistant account, or Google sign-in on these pages. The owner authorizes each Google account through Google's own consent screen.

This information site does not claim Google verification, endorsement, or a permanent authorization lifetime.
